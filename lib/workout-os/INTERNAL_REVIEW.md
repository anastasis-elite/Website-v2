# AOS Workout OS — Internal Review Standard

AOS is an assistive intelligence, not an autonomous clinician. It personalizes programming using available inputs; it does not diagnose or replace qualified judgment.

Qualified humans must periodically review assessment photos, pain flags, substitutions, performance, and outcomes for quality assurance and to confirm that intended movement adaptations are occurring. Structural labels and diagnostic-sounding explanations remain internal and must never be displayed to clients.

Corrective principles are a temporary bridge toward unrestricted strength, capability, confidence, resilience, movement quality, and long-term athleticism. They must never become the client’s program identity or a permanent ceiling.
